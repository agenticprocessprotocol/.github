## Agentic Process Protocol

The Agentic Process Protocol (APP) is a draft open specification for governing enterprise business processes that span AI agents, people, and business systems.

APP addresses what the enterprise still needs to prove across the whole process: who had authority, what the AI was allowed to do, whether required human review was substantive, what actually happened, and whether the governing rules changed.

APP sits alongside MCP and A2A. MCP addresses how agents connect to tools. A2A addresses how agents communicate with other agents. APP addresses how a cross-system enterprise process is specified, executed, evidenced, and improved.

### Status

APP is a draft specification. It is not yet hosted by a standards body. The Interim Custodian is Vach AI Limited, pending matriculation to a neutral host foundation.
